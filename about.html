Background

Who I Am

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at UC San Diego specializing in Controls and Robotics. I enjoy working on projects where hardware, software, and real-world testing all come together.

I first got into engineering through rocketry, where I learned how much effort goes into making a system reliable before it ever leaves the ground. That experience shaped the way I approach problems: understand the physics, build carefully, test often, and keep improving.

Most of my interests now center around embedded systems, control systems, avionics, and autonomous hardware. I like building things that have to sense, respond, and work reliably outside of a perfect lab environment.

Approach

How I Work

01

Understand the System

Before jumping into code, CAD, or PCB layout, I try to understand the physics behind the problem. Knowing what the system is actually doing makes every design decision more intentional.

02

Build, Test, Improve

I learn best by getting hardware in front of me and testing it early. Bench testing and field work have taught me how quickly real-world conditions reveal problems that are easy to miss on paper.

03

Connect Hardware and Software

I enjoy working across the full system, from schematics and PCB layout to firmware, controls, and live data. Being involved in each layer helps me catch assumptions before they turn into failures.
